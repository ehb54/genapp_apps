About Authors

SOMO (SOlution MOdeller; Rai et al. in Structure 13:723-734, 2005) bead modeling suite was originally implemented within one of the most widely used analytical ultracentrifugation data analysis software packages, UltraScan, as a standalone GUI application.

Developers:
