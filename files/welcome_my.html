Welcome

Welcome to NAMDrunner, a GUI framework for submitting biomolecular MM/MD simulations to remote compute resources!

On the basis of uploaded initial coordinates, PSF and parameter files, NAMDrunner submits and distibutes MM/MD simulation jobs over computational resources such as local clusters, supercomputers, computational grids and clouds. Features include: user management and statistics; job managements with multiple simultaneous reattachment to job running or run; integrated feedback mechanism which links full failed job information for easy debugging. Results of MD simulation runs (coordinate, velocity and trajectory files) are availabe for download.
