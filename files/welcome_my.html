Welcome

Welcome to ParamMD, a framework for building, parametrizing and running biomolecular MD simulations!

ParamMD is a versatile software suite for parametrizing biomolecular sructures and running subsequent molecular mechanics/molecular dynamics simulations. On the basis of uploaded .pdb structure(s), the software is capable of detecting the type of the molecule (proteins, nucleic acids, lipids etc.), its proper parametrization based on selected force field (AMBER, CHARMM, GROMOS etc), generation of the input files intended for MM/MD simulations, and running these simulations utilizing a variety of MD simulation programs such as NAMD, CHARMM, LAMMPS and others. The framework will be continuously updated with new features, including MD trajectory analysis tools, generation and parametrization of models of different resolution (coarse-graining) and many more.
