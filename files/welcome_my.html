Welcome to US-SOMO bead modeller/hydrodymanic calculation module!

US-SOMO module is a software framework for generating coarse-grained bead models from uploaded atomistic PDB structures, and subsequent calculation of hydrodynamic parameters on these models.

If you use US-SOMO bead modeller/hydrodynamic calculation module in your work, please cite:
