About Authors

WillItFit was developed by a group of scientists from University of Copenhagen, Dr. Martin Cramer Pedersen, Dr. Lise Arleth, and Dr. Kell Mortensen (from left to right, pictured below).

Dr. Pedersen is an associate at the Niels Bohr Institute, University of Copenhagen;

Dr. Arleth is a Professor MSO in Scattering and Synthetic Biology, Faculty of Science at the Niels Bohr Institute, University of Copenhagen;

Dr. Mortensen is the head of the X-ray and Neutron Science Section, Professor, at the Niels Bohr Institute, University of Copenhagen.
